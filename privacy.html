Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026
The short version: we only collect your email address when you join the waitlist. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than keeping you updated on DataQA. That's it.

Who we are

DataQA is a data quality tool currently in pre-launch. We are an independent, privately operated product. You can reach us at dataqa.app@protonmail.com.

What we collect

During our pre-launch waitlist phase, the only personal information we collect is your email address when you voluntarily submit it via the waitlist form on our website.

We do not collect your name, location, payment information, or any other personal data at this stage.

Why we collect it

Your email address is collected solely to:

  • Notify you when DataQA launches
  • Share early access information and updates
  • Communicate founder pricing or beta offers we've promised

We will not email you for any other purpose without your explicit consent.

Who we share it with

Your email is processed by Formspree (formspree.io), the third-party form service we use to collect waitlist submissions. Formspree stores your email securely and forwards it to us. You can read Formspree's privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your email address with any other third parties, advertisers, or marketing platforms.

How long we keep it

We retain your email address until you ask us to remove it, or until DataQA has launched and you have had the opportunity to create or decline an account.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your email from our waitlist at any time
  • Ask what data we hold about you
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you

To exercise any of these rights, email us at dataqa.app@protonmail.com and we will action your request within 5 business days.

Cookies

Our landing page does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party analytics at this stage. Basic browser behaviour (like scroll position) is handled entirely client-side and is not transmitted to us.

Changes to this policy

As DataQA grows, this policy will be updated to reflect new features and data practices. We will always post the updated date at the top of this page. If we make significant changes, we will notify waitlist members by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at dataqa.app@protonmail.com. We're a small team and we respond to every email.
